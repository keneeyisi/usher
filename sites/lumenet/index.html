FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Check availability at your address

How to find out which services you can order at your address. Enter your postcode and select your address; we show every plan you can order and the expected speed range.

Read the full guide: Check availability at your address

Minimum speed guarantee

The lowest speed we guarantee at your address and what happens if you are below it. Your guaranteed minimum download speed is shown in your order confirmation and in My Lumenet.

Read the full guide: Minimum speed guarantee

Move your service

How to transfer your broadband, TV and mobile bundle to a new address. Enter your new postcode in My Lumenet or call us; we show what is available at the new property.

Read the full guide: Move your service

Slow connection

Steps to take when your broadband is slower than usual or slower than your plan. Restart your Hub, test with a cable, and close other devices using the connection.

Read the full guide: Slow connection

Annual price rises

How and when prices change each year, and what notice we give you. Each April, prices rise by a fixed amount in pounds and pence, stated in your contract.

Read the full guide: Annual price rises

Router admin and Wi-Fi password

How to find, change or recover your Wi-Fi name and password and the Hub admin login. The Wi-Fi name and password are on the label under your Hub.

Read the full guide: Router admin and Wi-Fi password
