Service status

1Check first

Try a different socket and plug; if the Hub still has no lights, it may need replacing.

2Free replacement

Faulty equipment under 24 months old is replaced free of charge within two working days.

3Damage by you

Equipment damaged through misuse is charged at the replacement rate.

4Collection

We include a returns bag with the new item for the old one.
