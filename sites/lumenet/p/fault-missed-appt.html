Service status

1If we miss the appointment

You will receive £30 compensation automatically for each missed or cancelled-at-the-door visit.

2If you miss it

A new visit is arranged and a £25 fee may apply for a missed appointment without 24 hours' notice.

3Claiming

Report a missed visit in My Lumenet within 30 days; compensation appears as a credit on your next bill.

4Late engineers

If an engineer is more than two hours late, contact us and we will rebook with priority.
