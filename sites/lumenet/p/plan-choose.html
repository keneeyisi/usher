Service status

1How many people use it

One or two people can use 50Mbps; a household of four should look at 150Mbps or more.

2What you do online

Streaming in HD needs about 5Mbps per stream; video calls and gaming add to the load.

3Comparing plans

View all plans side by side by speed, price, minimum term and included extras.

4Changing plan later

You can upgrade at any time; downgrading before the end of your term may attract a fee.
