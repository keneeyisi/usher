Service status

1Allowance

Your plan shows the monthly data in GB; unlimited plans have a fair-use limit of 600GB.

2Running out

Buy a data boost, upgrade your plan or wait until your allowance resets on your billing date.

3Speed after fair use

After a fair-use limit, speeds may be reduced at peak times.

4Rollover

Unused data can roll over for a month on selected plans.
