Service status

1Your guaranteed minimum

Your guaranteed minimum download speed is shown in your order confirmation and in My Lumenet.

2If your speed is below the guarantee

Run three wired speed tests and contact us; we investigate and fix the fault within 30 days.

3If we cannot fix it

You can leave without paying early termination charges within the first 90 days or after the fix window.

4Exceptions

The guarantee excludes Wi-Fi speeds and problems caused by your own equipment.
