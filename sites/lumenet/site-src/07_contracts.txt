#con-terms | Your contract and minimum term | contract | What your contract says, how long the minimum term is and what you agreed to. | contract length; minimum term; terms and conditions; 24 month contract | How long am I tied in for ;; Finding out what I signed up to
- Minimum term :: Most plans have a 12 or 24-month minimum term starting from the day your service goes live.
- What is included :: Your plan, price, speed, any promotions and the equipment you receive.
- Contract summary :: Download your contract summary in My Lumenet under Documents.
- Cooling-off :: You have 14 days after ordering to cancel without charge, unless service has started.
#con-end-date | When your contract ends | contract | How to find your end date and what happens when your minimum term finishes. | contract end date; out of contract; renewal; contract expired | My contract just ended and I don't know what happens now ;; Working out the date I can leave without paying
- Finding the date :: See your end date in My Lumenet under Plan and contract.
- Reminder :: We tell you 40 to 60 days before the end date what your options are.
- If you do nothing :: You stay on a rolling plan at the out-of-contract price, which is usually higher.
- Renewing :: Choose a new deal or a rolling 30-day plan; there is no charge to switch.
#con-pause | Pausing your service | contract | Options when you are away for a long time or cannot use your service. | pause broadband; suspend service; going away; holiday hold | I'm away for a month, can I stop paying ;; Putting my broadband on hold while I am abroad
- Service holds :: A hold of up to three months is available on broadband for £5 a month, once per year.
- Mobile holds :: Mobile SIMs can be suspended for up to six months for £2 a month.
- What a hold does not do :: The minimum term does not stop counting during a hold, and TV cannot be paused.
- How to apply :: Request in My Lumenet at least seven days before you go.
#con-change-plan | Changing plan during your contract | contract | How to upgrade or move to a different plan while still in a minimum term. | upgrade plan; downgrade; move to faster plan; change tariff | I want faster broadband but I'm in contract ;; Moving to a cheaper tariff part-way through
- Upgrading :: Upgrade at any time; a new minimum term of 18 or 24 months may apply.
- Downgrading :: A downgrade in the minimum term may need an early change fee, unless you are in financial difficulty.
- Price effect :: The new price applies from your next bill after the change.
- How to change :: Use Change plan in My Lumenet or call us.
#rise-annual | Annual price rises | rises | How and when prices change each year, and what notice we give you. | price increase; April rise; inflation rise; price change letter | I got a message saying my price is going up ;; Why the price goes up every spring
- How the rise works :: Each April, prices rise by a fixed amount in pounds and pence, stated in your contract.
- Notice we give :: We tell you at least 30 days before the rise takes effect, by email and text.
- Your right to leave :: Where a rise is not set out in pounds in your contract, you can leave without charge within 30 days.
- Checking your price :: Your new monthly price appears in My Lumenet under Plan and contract.
#rise-out-of-contract | Out-of-contract pricing | rises | Why prices change when your minimum term ends and how to avoid paying more. | price after contract; end of promotion; discount ended; rolling plan | My contract ended and the price went up ;; The cheap deal has finished and my bill is now higher
- What changes :: When your promotional price ends, you move to the standard price for your plan.
- Staying on the standard price :: There is no penalty for staying on the standard price, but it is often higher than new customer deals.
- Ways to pay less :: Move to a new deal, change to a cheaper plan or ask for a loyalty price.
- Reminders :: We send two reminders before your end date and show your new price in My Lumenet.
#rise-leave | Leaving after a price rise | rises | When a price rise gives you the right to leave your contract without paying. | leave after price increase; exit penalty free; cancel because price rose | The price went up and I want out without paying ;; My contract allows me to cancel if the price rises
- When you can leave :: If your price rises by more than your contract allows, you can leave without early termination charges.
- Time limit :: You must tell us within 30 days of the notice of the price rise.
- How to leave :: Contact us and say you wish to leave because of a price rise; we confirm your end date.
- Equipment :: Return equipment within 30 days to avoid charges.
#etc-charges | Early termination charges | termination | The fee for leaving before your minimum term ends and how it is calculated. | cancellation fee; exit fee; leaving early; break the contract | I want to leave but I'm still in my contract ;; How much it will cost to cancel before the end
- How the charge works :: You pay the monthly cost for each remaining month of the minimum term, less any savings.
- Handset balance :: If you have a phone plan, you also pay the remaining handset balance.
- Getting a quote :: See your exact charge in My Lumenet under Leaving us.
- Ways to reduce it :: Move to a cheaper plan or ask us to waive some of it if you are in difficulty.
#etc-exemptions | Leaving without a charge | termination | Situations in which you can cancel your contract without paying early termination charges. | free exit; no penalty; leave penalty free; moving abroad | Can I get out of my contract without being fined ;; Circumstances that let me cancel free
- Faults :: If we cannot fix a fault or a speed shortfall within the time allowed, you can leave without charge.
- Price rises :: Where the price rise is not fixed in pounds, you may leave without paying.
- Moving :: If we cannot supply your new address, there is no charge.
- Bereavement or care :: We waive charges on the death of the account holder or a move into residential care.
#etc-calculate | Working out what you owe | termination | How to see how much to pay to end your contract at any date. | exit quote; how much to leave; remaining months | A figure for how much I would pay if I left next month ;; Seeing what I owe at the end of the contract
- Your leaving quote :: Choose a leave date in My Lumenet to see your charge for that date.
- What is included :: Remaining plan months, TV, add-ons and any outstanding device payments.
- Paying the charge :: The charge appears on your final bill, payable by card or Direct Debit.
- Written confirmation :: Request a letter confirming your end date and the charge by secure message.
#sw-cancel | Cancel your service | switchaway | How to cancel broadband, TV or mobile, notice periods and what to do with equipment. | cancel broadband;end my contract;leave lumenet;stop service;cancel my broadband;stop my broadband service;close my account and leave | I want to leave and go with another company ;; How to close my account
- Giving notice :: Give 30 days' notice in My Lumenet or by phone; mobile needs a PAC code first.
- Cancelling part of a bundle :: Cancel TV or mobile separately; removing a part can change the price of the bundle.
- Final bill :: Your last bill includes any early charge and credit for payment made in advance.
- Returning equipment :: Return equipment within 30 days using the freepost bag we send.
