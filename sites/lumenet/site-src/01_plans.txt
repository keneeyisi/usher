#speed-mbps | What Mbps means | speeds | A plain explanation of megabits, download, upload and latency. | megabits per second; download vs upload; broadband jargon; ping | I don't understand what the numbers on the adverts mean ;; What download, upload and ping mean for everyday use
- Download speed :: How quickly data comes to you, which affects streaming, browsing and downloading files.
- Upload speed :: How fast data leaves your home, important for video calls, backups and posting videos.
- Latency :: The delay in milliseconds before a request gets a reply; lower is better for gaming and calls.
- Megabits and megabytes :: 8 megabits make a megabyte, so 100Mbps downloads about 12MB each second.
