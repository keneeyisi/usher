#help-complaints | Make a complaint | complaints | How to complain about service, billing or sales and what happens next. | complain; complaint form; unhappy with service | I want to complain about how I have been treated ;; Telling the company that something went wrong
- How to complain :: Use the complaint form in My Lumenet, phone or write to us with your account number and what went wrong.
- What we do :: We acknowledge your complaint within two working days and aim to resolve it in ten.
- Compensation :: If we got something wrong we may apologise, fix it and pay compensation.
- Our final response :: A final response letter or deadlock letter is sent after eight weeks at the latest.

- Ask for a review :: Reply to the final response and ask for a senior manager to review.
- Independent scheme :: Take your complaint to our free alternative dispute resolution service after eight weeks.
- Regulator information :: You can report problems with providers to Ofcom, which monitors complaints.
- Keep records :: Keep reference numbers, dates and names for any contact.

- Who qualifies :: Customers who are elderly, disabled, have a long-term health condition or rely on telecare.
- What you get :: Priority fault repair, a dedicated phone line and a free battery backup for phone service.
- Registering :: Apply in My Lumenet or call us; we may ask for evidence from a medical professional.
- During a power cut :: Landline services need a battery backup; mobile signal may remain available.
