#acct-login | Sign-in problems | login | What to do if you cannot sign in to My Lumenet or the app. | can't log in; locked out; login failed; two-step verification | I can't get into my account any more ;; The app keeps saying my details are wrong
- Check your details :: Use your email address or account number; passwords are case-sensitive.
- Locked out :: After five wrong attempts, accounts lock for 30 minutes.
- Two-step verification :: We send a six-digit code by text; if your number has changed, contact us.
- Register a new account :: Use your account number and postcode from a bill to register.
#acct-password | Reset your password | login | How to choose a new password and what makes a strong one. | forgot password; reset password; change password; password link | I have forgotten my password ;; Changing my login details because they are old
- Reset link :: Choose Forgotten password and we email a link valid for 30 minutes.
- Choosing a password :: Use at least 12 characters with a mix of words and numbers.
- Changing it while signed in :: Go to Security in My Lumenet and choose Change password.
- If you do not get the email :: Check spam and that you used the right email; contact us if it still fails.
#acct-hacked | Secure a hacked account | secure | What to do if someone else has been using your account or you think your login has been stolen. | account hacked; someone logged in; unauthorised access; stolen account | Someone is logging in to my account ;; I noticed changes on my account that I did not make
- Act straight away :: Change your password, sign out of all devices and turn on two-step verification.
- Contact us :: Tell us what you saw; we can lock the account while we investigate and reverse changes.
- Check your bank :: Look for unrecognised payments and tell your bank if card details may be at risk.
- Protect other accounts :: Change passwords for any other service where you use the same password.
#acct-scams | Scam calls, texts and emails | secure | How to spot fake messages that pretend to be from Lumenet and what to do. | scam text; phishing; fake call; nuisance call; suspicious email | A text said my broadband would be cut off unless I paid ;; A caller said they were from Lumenet and wanted remote access
- Signs of a scam :: Urgent threats, links to unfamiliar sites and requests for card details, passwords or remote access.
- What we will never do :: We never ask for your password or full card number, or ask you to install software to fix a fault.
- If you have responded :: Contact your bank, change passwords and report to Action Fraud.
- Report it :: Forward suspicious texts to 7726 and emails to the address on our website.
