Cover levels

  • Lifetime pet cover

    Our highest level of cover, renewing every year with a vet fee limit that resets annually.

  • Time-limited and maximum benefit cover

    Lower-cost cover that pays up to a set amount per condition or for a limited period.

  • Accident-only cover

    The most basic level, covering injuries from accidents but not illness.
