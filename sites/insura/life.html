Life cover

  • Level and decreasing term life cover

    Cover that pays a lump sum if you die within a set term, either level or reducing over time.

  • Whole of life cover

    Cover that pays out whenever you die, as long as premiums are paid.

  • Joint and family life cover

    One policy covering two people, paying out on the first or second death.

Critical illness

  • Critical illness cover

    A lump sum paid if you are diagnosed with a serious illness listed in the policy.

  • Children's critical illness cover

    Built-in cover paying a lump sum if your child is diagnosed with a specified condition.

Income protection

  • Income protection insurance

    Regular payments if you cannot work because of illness or injury.

  • Short-term income cover

    Payments for up to one or two years if you cannot work through accident, sickness or unemployment.
