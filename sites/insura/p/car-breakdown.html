Make a claim
01

What's covered

Roadside repair, recovery to a local garage or home and onward travel, depending on the level of breakdown cover chosen.

02

Levels of breakdown cover

Roadside only, national recovery and national recovery with at-home start are offered; each adds distance and call-out benefits.

03

What's not covered

Repairs after the roadside visit, parts, fuel, and breakdowns within the first 24 hours of the policy starting.

04

How to use your cover

Call the breakdown line on your card with your policy number and exact location; keep the vehicle somewhere safe while you wait.
