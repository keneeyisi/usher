Make a claim
01

Tell us when it is sold

Contact us on the day you sell or scrap the car to end or move your cover; do not just stop paying.

02

Transferring cover

We can transfer the policy to a replacement vehicle, and your no-claims discount stays with you.

03

Refund

A pro rata refund is paid for any unused cover, less admin fee, if there are no claims in the year.

04

Statutory off-road notification

If you keep a car off the road, tell the DVLA, and ask us about cover for storage.
