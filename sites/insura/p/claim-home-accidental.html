Accidental damage claim

How to claim for sudden accidental damage to your home or belongings.

On this page

  1. Check you have the cover
  2. Making your claim
  3. Repair or replace
  4. Excess

Not the right page?

Press / or use I’m here because… and describe what you need in your own words.

01

Check you have the cover

Accidental damage is optional; your schedule says whether it applies to buildings, contents or both.

02

Making your claim

Describe what happened and when; we may ask for photos and a repair estimate.

03

Repair or replace

We choose the most cost-effective option, repairing where possible and replacing where not.

04

Excess

The accidental damage excess shown in your policy documents applies to each claim.
