01

What's covered

Damage to walls, roof, floors, windows, fixed baths and kitchens, plus garages and fences, from insured causes such as fire and storm.

02

What's not covered

Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, faulty workmanship and damage you cause by neglecting maintenance.

03

Rebuild cost and sum insured

Your sum insured should equal the cost of rebuilding the property, not its market value.

04

Mortgage lender requirements

Most lenders require buildings cover from completion; send them your schedule from My Insura.
