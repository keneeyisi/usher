Make a claim
01

How refunds are calculated

Pro rata for the unexpired period, less any admin fee and any claims paid in the policy year.

02

When refunds are paid

Within 14 days of cancellation, to the bank account or card used for payment.

03

Credit on your account

Overpayments are held as a credit and used against your next instalment, or refunded on request.

04

Refund after a claim

If a total loss claim has been paid, no refund of the premium is usually due.
