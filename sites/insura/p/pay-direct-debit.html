The Direct Debit Guarantee

You are protected by the guarantee; we write to you ten days before the first collection.

02

Changing the date

Choose a new collection day in My Insura; we may collect two payments in the month of change.

03

Changing the bank account

Set up a new Direct Debit instruction in My Insura; it takes five working days to process.

04

Cancelling the Direct Debit

Contact us first; cancelling your Direct Debit without telling us could lead to policy cancellation.
