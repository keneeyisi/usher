Make a claim
01

Your 14 days

You have 14 calendar days from the day you receive your documents, or the start date if later, to cancel.

02

Refund during cooling-off

A refund of the premium for the unused period, less a charge for any time you were covered.

03

How to cancel

Contact us by phone, in My Insura or in writing; keep a note of when you cancelled.

04

Claims in the cooling-off period

If you make a claim, you may not get a full refund and the rest of the premium may be due.
