01

What counts

Any illness, injury or symptom your pet has had before the start date, whether or not it was diagnosed.

02

How we decide

We review the vet history for the last 24 months to identify existing conditions.

03

Bilateral conditions

If one side was affected, the other side is excluded too, for example a cruciate ligament in either knee.

04

Can an exclusion be removed

Some conditions can be reviewed after 24 months with no treatment or signs, with a vet's letter.
