Cover levels

Third party only

The minimum cover required by law, protecting you against claims from other people but not paying for your own car.

Make a claim
01

What's covered

Your legal liability for injury to other people and damage to their vehicles or property, up to the limits in your policy.

02

What's not covered

Any loss of or damage to your own vehicle, including theft, fire and damage you cause yourself.

03

Costs and excess

The lowest-priced level of cover, though some drivers find comprehensive is similar in price after rating factors are applied.

04

How to claim

Tell us promptly about any incident involving another party and do not admit liability or agree a settlement without us.
