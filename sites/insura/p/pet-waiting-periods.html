01

Illness waiting period

You cannot claim for illness in the first 14 days of the policy; accidents are covered from day one.

02

Specific waiting periods

A cruciate ligament injury may have a 30-day wait, and behavioural therapy a longer wait.

03

Continuing from another insurer

Ask us to accept transfer of your cover with no new waiting periods where eligible.

04

Cooling-off

You can cancel within 14 days of receiving your documents for a full refund if no claim has been made.
