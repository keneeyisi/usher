Make a claim
01

Time-limited cover

Pays vet fees for a condition for up to 12 months from the date treatment starts, after which it is excluded.

02

Maximum benefit cover

Pays up to a fixed amount per condition; once the limit is reached, no further claims for that condition.

03

Which suits your pet

Younger pets with a short lifespan can suit lower-cost cover; breeds prone to chronic problems may need lifetime.

04

Upgrading

You can usually upgrade at renewal, but conditions that began under the old policy may then be excluded.
