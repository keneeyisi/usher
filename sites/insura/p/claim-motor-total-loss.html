Motor claims

Total loss and settlement

How we value your car and pay out if it is beyond economic repair.

01

When a car is a total loss

When repair costs would exceed the market value, we declare it a total loss and offer a settlement.

02

How we value your vehicle

We use the pre-accident market value from trade guides, taking account of age, mileage and condition.

03

Salvage categories

Cat S and Cat N describe repairable vehicles that were written off; you may keep the car if you accept less.

04

If you disagree

Send evidence such as adverts for similar cars and ask us to review the valuation.
