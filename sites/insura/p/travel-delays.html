Make a claim
01

Delay payment

A fixed sum is payable after the first 12 hours of delay at the departure point, then for each further 12 hours.

02

Missed departure

We cover extra travel and accommodation costs if you miss a departure because of a vehicle breakdown or accident.

03

Evidence to keep

Get written confirmation of the delay from the airline or carrier and keep all receipts.

04

Airline compensation

Claiming from the airline under flight compensation rules is separate; we may ask whether you have done so.
