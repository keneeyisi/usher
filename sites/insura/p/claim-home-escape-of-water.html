Home claims

Escape of water claim

How to claim for damage caused by a burst pipe, leaking appliance or overflowing tank.

On this page

  1. What to do straight away
  2. Making your claim
  3. What happens next
  4. Evidence we need

Not the right page?

Press / or use I’m here because… and describe what you need in your own words.

01

What to do straight away

Turn off the water at the stopcock, switch off electricity if safe, move belongings, and take photos before clearing up.

02

Making your claim

Start online in My Insura or call our home claims line, with your policy number and a description of the damage.

03

What happens next

We may appoint a loss adjuster, arrange a leak detection engineer and agree drying-out and repairs.

04

Evidence we need

Photographs, plumber's report and receipts for emergency repairs and any items you want to claim for.
