Pet claims

Lost, stolen or straying pet

What we cover if your pet goes missing or is stolen, including advertising and reward costs.

01

Advertising and reward

We contribute to advertising, a reward for return and kennel costs, up to the limit in your schedule.

02

What to do first

Report to the police, local vets, rescue centres and microchip database.

03

If your pet is not found

After 60 days, we can pay the purchase price or value of the pet if theft is proven.

04

Evidence we need

Microchip registration, photos, and proof of ownership such as receipts or vet records.
