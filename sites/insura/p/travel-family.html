Who counts as a child

Children under 18, or under 23 in full-time education, may be included at no extra cost when travelling with an adult.

02

Group sizes

Groups of up to ten adults can be covered together provided everyone travels on the same itinerary.

03

Excess for families

One excess applies per person per claim; the schedule shows the amounts for each traveller.

04

Adding a child later

Contact us to add a new baby or foster child; there may be no additional premium.
