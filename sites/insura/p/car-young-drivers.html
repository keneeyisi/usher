01

Why young drivers pay more

Insurers rate drivers under 25 and those new to driving as higher risk, which increases premiums and excesses.

02

Learner driver cover

Short-term cover lets a learner practise in a family car for days or weeks without affecting the main policyholder's no-claims discount.

03

Pass Plus and driver training

Completing an approved course such as Pass Plus may qualify for a discount on some policies.

04

Ways to reduce the cost

Choosing a smaller engine, a higher voluntary excess, a telematics policy or being named on a parent's policy can help.
