01

Who can be named

Any licensed driver aged 17 or over, though their age, history and claims record can change your premium.

02

Adding or removing a named driver

Use My Insura to add or remove a driver; changes take effect on the date you choose and may adjust your price.

03

Driving other cars

Some policies include limited third-party cover for named drivers in cars they do not own; check your policy wording.

04

Named driver and no-claims discount

A named driver does not earn their own no-claims discount unless the policyholder is the main driver, and they should not be misrepresented as the main driver.
