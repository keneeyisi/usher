What's covered

Medical treatment for winter sports injuries, mountain rescue, lost ski pass and hired equipment.

02

Piste closure

A fixed daily amount is payable if lack of snow closes the piste for more than 24 hours.

03

Off-piste skiing

Off-piste is covered only when accompanied by a qualified guide and within the marked boundaries.

04

Adding winter sports

Choose the add-on at quote, or add it later through My Insura before departure.
