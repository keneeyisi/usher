Lost, stolen or damaged possessions claim

01

What to do abroad

Report theft to local police within 24 hours and loss to the airline or carrier in writing.

02

Proof you need

Police report, property irregularity report from the airline, and receipts or proof of ownership.

03

Limits and depreciation

We apply the single-item limit and a deduction for age and wear on belongings.

04

Passports and cash

Replacement of emergency travel documents and limited cash theft are covered, subject to evidence.
