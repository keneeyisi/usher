What's covered

Emergency medical costs, cancellation and curtailment, delayed baggage and personal liability for the dates of your trip.

02

Start and end dates

Cover for cancellation starts when you buy the policy; all other sections start on the trip departure date.

03

Trip length limits

Single trip policies cover trips up to 31 days in most cases; longer trips may need extended cover.

04

Buying after you have booked

Buy as soon as you book to be covered if you must cancel for an insured reason.
