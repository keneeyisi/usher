Make a claim
01

Signs of a scam

Unexpected calls asking for card details, texts with links to pay, or emails requesting a change of bank account.

02

What to do now

Stop contact, call your bank, change passwords and report to Action Fraud; then tell us.

03

Reporting to us

Forward suspicious emails to the address on our website and tell us the number or website used.

04

How we will contact you

We will never ask you for your full card number, PIN or password.
