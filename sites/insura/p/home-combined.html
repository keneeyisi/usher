01

Benefits of combining

One renewal date, one excess to pay in many cases, and a combined discount compared with buying separately.

02

When to choose separate policies

Tenants only need contents; owners with a leasehold flat may find the freeholder already insures the structure.

03

Excess on a combined claim

A single claim affecting both building and contents normally carries only one excess.

04

Switching to combined cover

Add buildings or contents through My Insura; your new schedule shows the combined sums insured.
