Car insurance

Cover for the car you drive: cover levels, add-ons, drivers, no-claims discount and telematics.

Cover levels

  • Comprehensive cover

    Our highest level of motor cover, protecting your own car as well as other people's property.

  • Third party, fire and theft

    A middle level of cover that insures damage you cause to others plus loss of your car through fire or theft.

  • Third party only

    The minimum cover required by law, protecting you against claims from other people but not paying for your own car.

  • Excesses explained

    The compulsory and voluntary excess are the amounts you pay towards a claim before we pay the rest.

Add-ons

  • Breakdown cover

    An optional add-on that sends a patrol or recovery vehicle if your car breaks down or will not start.

  • Courtesy car

    An optional add-on providing a small replacement vehicle while your car is being repaired after a claim.

  • Motor legal expenses

    An optional add-on helping with legal costs to pursue uninsured losses after an accident that was not your fault.

  • Windscreen cover

    Cover for repair or replacement of damaged windscreens and windows, which usually carries a reduced excess.

Drivers and use

  • Named drivers

    Adding other people to your policy so they can drive your car with your insurance in place.

  • Young and newly qualified drivers

    How cover works for drivers under 25 or who passed their test in the last two years.

  • Business use and commuting

    What counts as social, commuting or business use of your car and how this affects cover and price.

  • Driving abroad

    Cover for taking your car to other countries, including green card requirements and breakdown abroad.

No-claims discount

  • How your no-claims discount works

    The discount you earn for each claim-free year, and what happens to it if you make a claim.

  • Proof of your no-claims discount

    How to get a letter or document showing how many years of claim-free driving you have earned.

  • Protected no-claims discount

    An add-on that lets you make a limited number of claims without losing your discount.

Telematics

  • Black box and app-based insurance

    Insurance where a device or smartphone app measures how you drive and rewards safer habits with a lower price.
