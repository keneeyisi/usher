#travel-single-trip | Single trip cover | single | Cover for one holiday or journey, from the day you book until you return. | one-off holiday insurance; single trip policy | Insurance for just one holiday this summer ;; Cover for a single break away that I have booked
- What's covered :: Emergency medical costs, cancellation and curtailment, delayed baggage and personal liability for the dates of your trip.
- Start and end dates :: Cover for cancellation starts when you buy the policy; all other sections start on the trip departure date.
- Trip length limits :: Single trip policies cover trips up to 31 days in most cases; longer trips may need extended cover.
- Buying after you have booked :: Buy as soon as you book to be covered if you must cancel for an insured reason.

- What's covered :: Every trip you start in the policy year, up to the maximum trip length shown in your schedule, in the countries you selected.
- Maximum trip length :: Typically 31 or 45 days per trip; longer trips need an extension or a separate policy.

#travel-medical | Emergency medical cover | medical | Cover for urgent medical treatment, hospital stays and repatriation if you are ill or injured abroad. | medical expenses abroad; hospital abroad; repatriation | I got ill on holiday and ended up in hospital abroad ;; Paying for treatment and bringing me home if I am hurt overseas
- What's covered :: Hospital treatment, doctor fees, ambulance, and medical repatriation to the UK, up to the medical limit on your policy.
- Calling the emergency assistance team :: Contact our 24-hour team before incurring large costs or agreeing to treatment, so we can arrange payment directly.
- GHIC and EHIC :: A UK Global Health Insurance Card gives access to state treatment in the EU but does not cover private care or repatriation.
- What's not covered :: Treatment that could wait until you return home, and costs of routine or planned procedures.

- What counts as pre-existing :: Any medical condition you have been diagnosed with, treated for or had symptoms of before the policy starts.
- How to declare :: Answer the medical screening questions honestly; you may be offered cover with an extra premium or exclusions.
- If your health changes :: Tell us if you receive a new diagnosis or change treatment between buying and travelling.
- What happens if you do not declare :: A claim relating to an undeclared condition can be reduced or refused.
#travel-cancellation | Cancellation and curtailment | medical | Cover for the cost of a trip you must cancel or cut short for an insured reason. | trip cancelled; cut holiday short; cancelled flight refund | I had to call off our holiday because of illness ;; Getting back the deposit when something stops us travelling
- What's covered :: Unrecoverable travel and accommodation costs if you cannot travel or must return early because of illness, bereavement or jury service.
- Cancelling for illness :: A doctor's certificate is needed, and you should cancel the trip as soon as you know you cannot travel.
- Insured reasons :: Examples include serious illness or injury, redundancy, and damage to your home that needs you at home.
- Not covered :: Changing your mind, financial hardship that is not redundancy, and most airline strikes known before you book.
#travel-delays | Travel delay and missed departure | medical | Compensation for long delays and for missing a departure because of an unexpected event. | flight delay compensation; missed flight; delayed departure | The flight was delayed for ten hours and I was stuck at the airport ;; I missed my plane because of a traffic jam
- Delay payment :: A fixed sum is payable after the first 12 hours of delay at the departure point, then for each further 12 hours.
- Missed departure :: We cover extra travel and accommodation costs if you miss a departure because of a vehicle breakdown or accident.
- Evidence to keep :: Get written confirmation of the delay from the airline or carrier and keep all receipts.
- Airline compensation :: Claiming from the airline under flight compensation rules is separate; we may ask whether you have done so.
#travel-baggage | Baggage and personal possessions | medical | Cover for lost, stolen or damaged luggage and items such as phones and cameras. | lost luggage; stolen phone abroad; delayed baggage | My suitcase never arrived and my phone got nicked on holiday ;; Paying for replacement of damaged or stolen luggage
- What's covered :: Loss, theft or damage to baggage and personal possessions, with a single-item limit and an overall limit.
- Delayed baggage :: Emergency purchases of toiletries and clothes are covered if your bags are delayed for more than 12 hours.
- Valuables and gadgets :: Items such as cameras and phones have special limits and must not be left unattended.
- Reporting loss :: Tell the police within 24 hours of theft and the airline of loss in writing, and keep reports.
