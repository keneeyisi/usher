#claim-home-escape-of-water | Escape of water claim | homeclaims | How to claim for damage caused by a burst pipe, leaking appliance or overflowing tank. | water damage claim;burst pipe claim;leak claim | A pipe burst and my ceiling came down, how do I start a claim ;; Making a claim for water that leaked through my floor ;; Something at home has burst or leaked and I need to report the damage ;; Where to send pictures of the damage for a home claim
- What to do straight away :: Turn off the water at the stopcock, switch off electricity if safe, move belongings, and take photos before clearing up.
- Making your claim :: Start online in My Insura or call our home claims line, with your policy number and a description of the damage.
- What happens next :: We may appoint a loss adjuster, arrange a leak detection engineer and agree drying-out and repairs.
- Evidence we need :: Photographs, plumber's report and receipts for emergency repairs and any items you want to claim for.
#claim-home-burglary | Burglary claim | homeclaims | How to report a break-in and claim for stolen or damaged items. | theft from home claim;break-in claim;stolen belongings claim | Thieves took my laptop and TV and I want to claim ;; Reporting a break in at home and getting stolen items replaced ;; Someone broke into my house and took things and I need to report it
- What to do first :: Report the burglary to the police and get a crime reference number; do not clean up until we advise.
- Making your claim :: Tell us the crime reference number and list what was stolen with approximate dates and values.
- Proof of ownership :: Receipts, photographs, serial numbers and bank statements help us settle your claim quickly.
- Replacement and cash settlement :: We usually replace items through our suppliers or pay a cash sum if we cannot replace them.
#claim-home-storm-flood | Storm and flood claim | homeclaims | How to claim for damage from storm, flooding or snow. | roof damage claim; flood claim; fallen tree claim | A tree came down on my roof in the storm ;; My downstairs flooded after heavy rain and I want to claim
- Make the property safe :: Prevent further damage with temporary repairs, such as tarpaulins, and keep receipts.
- Making your claim :: Claim online or by phone as soon as you can; in a widespread event, claims lines may be busy.
- Flood restoration :: We arrange drying and cleaning and may pay for alternative accommodation while the property is repaired.
- Fallen trees and fences :: A tree falling on a building is covered; a tree that has damaged only a fence or hedge may not be.
#claim-home-accidental | Accidental damage claim | homeclaims | How to claim for sudden accidental damage to your home or belongings. | broken window claim; spilled paint claim; damaged TV claim | I dropped my brand new TV and cracked the screen ;; Making a claim when something got smashed by accident
- Check you have the cover :: Accidental damage is optional; your schedule says whether it applies to buildings, contents or both.
- Making your claim :: Describe what happened and when; we may ask for photos and a repair estimate.
- Repair or replace :: We choose the most cost-effective option, repairing where possible and replacing where not.
- Excess :: The accidental damage excess shown in your policy documents applies to each claim.
#claim-motor-accident | Accident claim | motorclaims | What to do after a crash, and how to report it and start a motor claim. | car crash claim; collision claim; report an accident | I had a crash at a junction and the other driver was at fault ;; What to do right after I have been in a car accident
- At the scene :: Check everyone is safe, call 999 if anyone is injured, exchange details and take photographs; do not admit fault.
- Reporting to us :: Report the accident through My Insura or our 24-hour motor line as soon as possible, even if you do not intend to claim.
- Fault and recovering costs :: If the other driver is at fault we try to recover our costs and your excess from their insurer.
- Repairs :: We recommend an approved repairer who guarantees repairs, or you may choose your own garage with our agreement.
#claim-motor-theft | Theft of your vehicle claim | motorclaims | What to do if your car has been stolen or broken into. | stolen car; car theft claim; car broken into | My car was stolen from outside my house ;; Someone smashed my window and took the satnav
- Report to the police :: Report the theft immediately and get a crime reference number before contacting us.
- Waiting period :: We wait up to 28 days for a stolen vehicle to be recovered before treating it as a total loss.
- Keys and documents :: We will ask for all sets of keys, the V5C logbook and details of any security devices.
- If the car is recovered :: Do not drive it; we will arrange recovery and inspection and, if repairable, a repair.
#claim-motor-hit-and-run | Hit and run or uninsured driver | motorclaims | What to do if someone hits your car and drives away or has no insurance. | hit and run; no insurance other driver; someone drove into my parked car | Someone drove into my parked car and didn't leave a note ;; The other driver did not stop after hitting me
- What to do :: Report the incident to the police within 24 hours and note the registration if you saw it; take photographs of the damage.
- Making your claim :: Call our motor claims line with the police reference; we handle the claim under your own policy.
- Uninsured Driver Agreement :: Where the other driver is traced as uninsured, we may recover costs through the Motor Insurers' Bureau.
- No-claims discount :: If you are not at fault and we recover costs, your no-claims discount is normally unaffected.
#claim-motor-windscreen | Windscreen claim | motorclaims | How to report and book a windscreen repair or replacement. | glass claim; chipped windscreen claim; book windscreen repair | A chip appeared in my windscreen on the motorway ;; Getting a cracked car window repaired through my policy
- Before you book :: Check you have glass cover on your schedule; some policies include it automatically with comprehensive cover.
- Book your repair :: Call our glass partner or use My Insura; mobile fitters can visit your home or work.
- Excess to pay :: Pay the glass excess at the time of repair; repair is usually free of excess if it is a chip repair.
- Effect on your no-claims discount :: A glass-only claim should not reduce your no-claims discount under comprehensive cover.
#claim-motor-total-loss | Total loss and settlement | motorclaims | How we value your car and pay out if it is beyond economic repair. | write-off; category S N; car written off; settlement value | My car is a write-off and I want to know how much I will get ;; Disagreeing with the valuation of my written off car
- When a car is a total loss :: When repair costs would exceed the market value, we declare it a total loss and offer a settlement.
- How we value your vehicle :: We use the pre-accident market value from trade guides, taking account of age, mileage and condition.
- Salvage categories :: Cat S and Cat N describe repairable vehicles that were written off; you may keep the car if you accept less.
- If you disagree :: Send evidence such as adverts for similar cars and ask us to review the valuation.
#claim-travel-medical | Medical expenses abroad claim | travelclaims | How to claim for treatment or repatriation after illness or injury abroad. | claim hospital bill abroad;medical claim travel;ill on holiday claim | I had to see a doctor in Spain and want my money back ;; Claiming for an ambulance and hospital stay on holiday ;; I needed hospital treatment abroad and want the costs paid back
- Before you incur costs :: Call the emergency assistance team on your card for any hospital admission or if costs may exceed £500.
- Documents needed :: Itemised invoices, receipts, medical reports and your passport or booking confirmation.
- Settlement :: We pay you or the hospital directly and deduct any excess shown on your schedule.
- Claims on return home :: Complete the online travel claim form within 31 days of your return.
#claim-travel-possessions | Lost, stolen or damaged possessions claim | travelclaims | How to claim for luggage, phones, cameras and cash lost or stolen on a trip. | phone stolen on holiday;lost luggage claim;baggage claim | My phone got nicked on holiday and I need to claim ;; The airline lost my suitcase and I want to claim for what was in it ;; Something I took on holiday was stolen or lost and I need to report it
- What to do abroad :: Report theft to local police within 24 hours and loss to the airline or carrier in writing.
- Proof you need :: Police report, property irregularity report from the airline, and receipts or proof of ownership.
- Limits and depreciation :: We apply the single-item limit and a deduction for age and wear on belongings.
- Passports and cash :: Replacement of emergency travel documents and limited cash theft are covered, subject to evidence.
#claim-travel-cancellation | Cancellation or curtailment claim | travelclaims | How to claim after you cancel or cut short a trip for an insured reason. | cancel holiday claim; trip cut short claim; refund of holiday costs | We had to cancel because my mother fell ill and I want the money back ;; Claiming for the flights I paid for but could not use
- Tell your travel providers :: Contact airlines and hotels straight away to cancel and ask what refunds are available.
- Documents needed :: Booking confirmations, receipts, a doctor's letter and proof of unused travel costs.
- Medical certificates :: For illness, your doctor must complete the medical certificate in our claim form.
- Settlement :: We pay the unrecoverable amount up to the cancellation limit, less the excess.

- What you need :: The completed claim form, itemised vet invoice and clinical history from the vet.

#claim-pet-lost | Lost, stolen or straying pet | petclaims | What we cover if your pet goes missing or is stolen, including advertising and reward costs. | missing pet; dog stolen; lost cat | My dog has gone missing and I do not know what to do ;; Advertising for a lost pet and claiming reward costs
- Advertising and reward :: We contribute to advertising, a reward for return and kennel costs, up to the limit in your schedule.
- What to do first :: Report to the police, local vets, rescue centres and microchip database.
- If your pet is not found :: After 60 days, we can pay the purchase price or value of the pet if theft is proven.
- Evidence we need :: Microchip registration, photos, and proof of ownership such as receipts or vet records.
#claim-track | Track your claim | after | How to see progress, updates and next steps on a claim you have made. | claim status; where is my claim; update on my claim | I made a claim last week and have not heard anything ;; Checking what stage my claim has reached
- Where to see status :: Sign in to My Insura and open Open claims to see progress, dates and any documents we need.
- What the stages mean :: Registered, under review, assessor booked, repair authorised, settlement agreed and closed.
- Typical timescales :: We acknowledge claims within one working day and aim to update you at least every ten working days.
- Contacting your claims handler :: Use secure messages in My Insura or the number on your claim reference letter.
#claim-assessor | Assessor visits and approved repairers | after | How loss adjusters, engineers and repair partners deal with your claim. | loss adjuster visit; engineer inspection; repair appointment | Waiting for someone to come and look at the damage ;; Who will come and inspect my property or car
- Assessor visits :: A loss adjuster or engineer may visit to inspect damage and agree the scope of repairs.
- Approved repairers :: Our repair partners guarantee workmanship for 12 months and handle the claim paperwork for you.
- Choosing your own repairer :: You can use your own, but we must agree the estimate before work begins.
- If an appointment is missed :: Contact us to rearrange; contact details are on the appointment email.
#claim-settlement | Settlement offers and payments | after | How a claim is valued, offered and paid, including cash settlement and excess. | claim payout; settlement offer; how much will I get | The insurer has made an offer and I do not know if it is fair ;; How and when my claim money is paid
- How we value your claim :: We pay the cost of repair or replacement, minus any excess and an allowance for wear and tear on some items.
- Cash settlement :: We may offer cash instead of repair at the cost to us of carrying out the work.
- When you will be paid :: Payment is made within five working days of agreeing the settlement and receiving your bank details.
- Underinsurance :: If your sum insured is too low, the claim may be reduced in proportion.
#claim-dispute | If you disagree with a claim decision | after | How to ask for a review or complain if your claim is declined or you are unhappy with an offer. | claim declined; appeal claim decision; unhappy with settlement | My claim was turned down and I think that is wrong ;; Challenging a low offer on my claim
- Ask for a review :: Send us your reasons and any new evidence; a different claims manager will review the decision.
- Make a complaint :: You can complain at any time; we will respond within eight weeks.
- Financial Ombudsman Service :: If you are still unhappy after our final response, you may take your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service free of charge.
- Legal action :: You also have the right to seek legal advice; time limits apply.
