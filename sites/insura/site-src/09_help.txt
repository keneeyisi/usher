- How to complain :: Tell us by phone, post, secure message or complaint form; give your reference and what went wrong.
- What we do :: We acknowledge within three working days and aim to resolve within eight weeks, keeping you updated.
- Final response :: When we reach a final response we tell you the outcome and your right to go to the Ombudsman.
- Compensation :: Where we have made a mistake we may offer an apology, put things right and pay compensation for distress or costs.
#help-ombudsman | Financial Ombudsman Service | complaints | Taking a complaint to the independent Ombudsman after our final response. | ombudsman; independent review; escalate complaint | I am still not satisfied after your final answer ;; Taking my dispute to an independent body for free
- When you can go :: After our final response, or after eight weeks if we have not given a final response.
- Time limits :: Go within six months of our final response; the Ombudsman may take older complaints in some cases.
- How it works :: The service is free and independent; it reviews the case and may order us to pay compensation.
- Contact details :: Financial Ombudsman Service, fictional contact details are shown on your final response letter.
#help-fraud-report | Report suspicious contact | fraud | What to do if someone has pretended to be us or you think you have been scammed. | scam; phishing; fake call; impersonation | I think someone pretending to be you scammed me ;; I got a text asking me to pay a fee for my policy
- Signs of a scam :: Unexpected calls asking for card details, texts with links to pay, or emails requesting a change of bank account.
- What to do now :: Stop contact, call your bank, change passwords and report to Action Fraud; then tell us.
- Reporting to us :: Forward suspicious emails to the address on our website and tell us the number or website used.
- How we will contact you :: We will never ask you for your full card number, PIN or password.
#help-fraud-claims | Insurance fraud and false claims | fraud | What counts as insurance fraud and what happens when it is found. | false claim; lying on application; staged accident; ghost broker | A friend offered to get me cheap car insurance through someone they know ;; What happens if someone exaggerates a claim
- What is insurance fraud :: Deliberately giving false information, exaggerating a claim or staging an accident to obtain money.
- Ghost brokers :: People who sell fake policies at low prices; check details on the regulator's register before paying.
- What we do :: We investigate suspected fraud, may refuse claims, cancel policies and share information with fraud databases.
- Reporting fraud :: Tell us in confidence if you suspect someone of fraud; calls are free and anonymous if you wish.
