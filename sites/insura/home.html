Buildings

  • Buildings insurance

    Cover for the structure of your home: walls, roof, floors, fixed fittings and outbuildings.

Contents

  • Contents insurance

    Cover for belongings inside your home such as furniture, electronics, clothes and carpets.

  • Buildings and contents together

    A single policy covering both the building and what is inside it, often at a lower combined price.

What's covered

  • Escape of water

    Cover for damage from water that escapes from pipes, tanks or appliances.

  • Accidental damage

    Optional protection for sudden, unexpected damage to your home or belongings that is not covered elsewhere.

  • Theft and burglary

    Cover for items stolen from your home and damage caused by thieves breaking in.

  • Storm, flood and weather

    Cover for damage to your home from storms, flooding, snow and heavy rainfall.

  • Subsidence, heave and landslip

    Cover for movement of the ground beneath your home that damages the building.

  • Frozen pipes and heating

    Cover for damage caused when pipes freeze and burst, and conditions about keeping your home heated.

  • Alternative accommodation

    Help with living costs if you cannot stay in your home after an insured incident.

Optional extras

  • Personal possessions outside the home

    Optional cover for valuables such as phones, cameras and bikes when you take them out.

  • Home emergency and legal cover

    Optional add-ons for emergency call-outs such as boiler failure and for legal disputes about your home.

Landlords and renters

  • Landlord insurance

    Cover for owners who let property, including buildings, contents and loss of rent.

  • Renters' contents insurance

    Cover for tenants for their own belongings and for accidental damage to a landlord's fixtures.
