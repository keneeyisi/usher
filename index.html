Method

How the approach works

Usher connects the language people naturally use with the information and navigation structures presented by a website.

01

Understand the request

The user describes what they need in everyday language, without having to know the website's exact labels or categories.

02

Connect intent to website content

Usher interprets the request and relates it to relevant pages, information and available navigation options within the selected website.

03

Support the next step

The interface guides the user towards relevant content and actions, helping them continue their task within the website.

Research

Research context and contribution

Usher investigates the terminology gap between the way people express their goals and the language and structures used by complex websites.

◎

Research focus

Explore how natural-language interaction can help people express information needs and navigate complex website environments.

▱

Contribution

A design and implementation approach for intent-based website navigation, explored through three independent demonstrators.

▤

Demonstration

CivicSphere, Insura and Lumenet provide distinct contexts for examining the approach and its interaction patterns.

About

About the Usher project

Usher is a research project exploring natural-language navigation for complex websites. The three demonstrators provide interactive environments for examining the approach across different service contexts.
